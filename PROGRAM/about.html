About DoBu

Our Story

Passion for Martial Arts

DoBu Martial Arts was founded in 2005 with a simple mission: to create a welcoming space where people of all ages and skill levels could discover the transformative power of martial arts.

What started as a small dojo has grown into a premier training facility offering a comprehensive range of martial arts disciplines, fitness training, and self-defence courses.

Our approach combines traditional martial arts values with modern training methodologies, creating an environment where discipline meets innovation.
